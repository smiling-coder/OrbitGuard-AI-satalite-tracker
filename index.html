Collision risk analyzer SCREENING ESTIMATE

Method: circular two-body propagation in an inertial frame, sampled every 20 s and refined to 1 s around the minimum. Distance alone is not a collision probability; no covariance is available, so none is computed.

Satellite manoeuvre simulator ILLUSTRATIVE · SIMULATION

Assumptions: circular orbits; a tangential burn changes the semi-major axis by about 2a·Δv/v (first order), and the orbit is then treated as circular again. Radial and normal burns, eccentricity and drag are not modelled. Propellant uses the rocket equation with your mass and Isp. This is a teaching comparison, not an operational manoeuvre plan, and no commands are ever sent.

Collision event simulator HYPOTHETICAL · SYNTHETIC FRAGMENTS

Choose two objects and run. The event happens at the current simulation time, at Object A's position (a hypothetical meeting point). Both parents are removed and replaced by synthetic fragments, which show in red in the 3D view.

Fragment model: each fragment gets random tangential and normal velocity changes (normal distribution) that shift its altitude and inclination. It is a classroom simplification and does not predict what a real collision would produce. "Close approaches" counts fragment/satellite pairs within the yellow distance from the Risk Analyzer over 6 h, after excluding pairs whose altitudes already differ by more than that distance.

AI trajectory predictor SIMULATION MODE · PREDICTION

A: physics propagation from catalogue values (ignores the hidden drag). B: experimental quadratic model fitted to the last 6 h of noisy simulated observations. C: baseline linear fit. Training data is past-only and errors are measured on the future (no leakage), against a simulated truth. This shows how methods compare, not real-world accuracy. No uncertainty bands are computed.

Debris detection lab SIMULATED SENSOR

Run the simulation.

One fixed ground sensor (15°N, 75°E), 10° minimum elevation. Detection chance is an illustrative model using size and range only; real detection also depends on sensor resolution, geometry, reflectivity and conditions. A webcam or a browser cannot detect real debris. The estimated path is a straight-line fit of orbital angle to the observations that were made.

Alert intelligence SIMULATION

Times are predicted from simulated orbits, not exact countdowns. Priority comes from miss distance against your thresholds. Status and notes are saved in this browser.

Atmospheric drag simulator EDUCATIONAL MODEL

Circular-orbit decay with a simple exponential atmosphere (400 km reference, scaled for low and high solar activity). Density really varies with altitude, solar activity, geomagnetic storms and time of day, so a constant density is shown only as an incomplete comparison. Treat results as illustrations.

Orbital data centre

Import a CSV or a TLE file (2-line or 3-line, e.g. downloaded from CelesTrak). TLE epoch and eccentricity are not modelled, so TLE objects are placed on the simplified circular model and are not real positions. CSV columns: id,type,alt_km,inc_deg,raan_deg,ma_deg (optional name). Altitudes in km, angles in degrees. OMM files are not supported and will be rejected.

Why debris matters

Space debris is human-made material in orbit that no longer serves a purpose. It forms when rockets and satellites break up, collide, or are left behind after their missions.

Objects move at several km/s, so even a fragment smaller than a coin can damage a satellite. Collisions create more fragments, which raises the chance of further collisions.

Collision avoidance means predicting close approaches and, when warranted, changing an orbit. End-of-life disposal moves satellites to a lower orbit or a graveyard orbit so they stop adding risk.

Everything in this app is an educational simulation, not a forecast.
